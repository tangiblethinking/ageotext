Implementation

3. Page-Specific Semantic Structures

A. The Product Listing Page (PLP) Structure

Establishes catalog breadth and category domain authority.

  • h1: Category Name only. Exactly one per page.
  • h2: Semantic Filters, Catalog Subsections, or Category FAQs.
  • h3: Curated sub-categories under an h2.
  • Grid Items: Use anchor or div + span.text-h4. Pair with ItemList JSON-LD. Do not repeat h3 tags.

B. The Product Detail Page (PDP) Structure

Feeds AI engines product specs, ingredients, clinical proof, and purchase intent.

  • h1: Full exact product title.
  • h2: Major blocks (Ingredients, How to Take, Clinical Results) as high-intent queries.
  • h3: Sub-attributes under h2.
  • h4–h6: Technical certifications and deep specs.
  • Disclaimers: Non-heading markup only.

C. Copy-Heavy Landing Pages & Editorial Content

Maps user problems to commercial solutions. Primary source for GEO citations.

  • h1: Editorial title with target entity.
  • h2: Conversational questions immediately followed by a p.text-body answer block.
  • h3: Recommended products or sub-steps.
  • h4: Active ingredients or clinical callouts.

Consensus & Authority

5. Off-Page Entity Consensus (The GEO Authority Layer)

On-page HTML hierarchy structures content for crawlers, but LLMs validate citations through cross-source entity consensus.

  1. Cross-Platform Consistency: Product names, ingredients, benefits, and claims stated identically across domain, press, social, and vendor listings.
  2. Third-Party Corroboration: Generative engines cross-reference against PubMed, journals, review sites, and forums. Maintain external digital PR.
